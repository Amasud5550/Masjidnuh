Setting up the TV

  1. Connect a small computer to the TV and to the internet.
  2. Open Chrome and go to
  3. Press F11 (Windows) or Ctrl+Cmd+F (Mac) for full screen.
  4. Turn off sleep and screen saver so it stays on.

The setup guide in README.md explains how to make it start by itself when the computer turns on.
